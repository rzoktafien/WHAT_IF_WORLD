# WHAT IF WORLD — IDEA SCORING SYSTEM V3

Score each dimension 0–5; weighted total is out of 100. Scores are editorial prioritization, not probabilities of reach or virality.

| Dimension | Weight | 0 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Evidence freshness/relevance | 10% | No signal and falsely presented as trend | Some credible contextual signal | Strong, repeated, current evidence |
| Scientific foundation | 25% | Contradicted/unsupported | Plausible, research still needed | Clear mechanism and primary support |
| Human stakes | 15% | Abstract/no consequence | Understandable consequence | Concrete, emotionally legible stakes |
| Visual originality | 20% | Generic/static | Some memorable imagery | Distinct, executable visual hook |
| Story potential | 15% | No causal escalation | Basic progression | Clear escalation, twist, payoff |
| Novelty vs archive | 10% | Duplicate | Moderate angle change | Materially distinct premise/angle |
| Indonesian relevance | 5% | No meaningful relevance | General relevance | Strong local evidence/context |

Formula: `sum(score / 5 * weight)`.

## Evidence labels
- `CURRENT_VERIFIED`: fresh, multi-source signal with traceable data.
- `CURRENT_LIMITED`: current signal but incomplete/indirect.
- `EVERGREEN`: not claimed to be trending.
- `ORIGINAL_EDITORIAL`: original question, scientifically researchable.

## Decision bands
- 85–100: priority candidate; still requires research gate.
- 70–84: viable with angle or evidence improvement.
- 55–69: backlog/research more.
- <55: do not recommend now.

## Non-compensatory blockers
Any of these blocks recommendation regardless of score:
- core premise conflicts with well-established science and cannot be reframed;
- no credible way to verify the core mechanism;
- duplicate without a new angle;
- likely harmful/misleading framing cannot be mitigated;
- purported trend has no traceable evidence.

Always show per-dimension scores, total, evidence label, reasons, and trade-off.

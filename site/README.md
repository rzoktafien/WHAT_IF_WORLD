# WHAT IF WORLD Website MVP

This is a static, responsive landing-page draft for the WHAT IF WORLD brand.

## Files
- index.html — semantic landing page in Indonesian.
- styles.css — responsive visual system.

## Current status
- Draft only; not deployed.
- Social links and business contact are intentionally omitted until official accounts and contact route are verified.
- No analytics, cookies, forms, or third-party trackers are included.
- The page does not claim published episodes, client work, audience metrics, or commercial rights clearance.

## Deployment prerequisites
1. Owner confirms final brand identity and domain.
2. Owner chooses hosting / CMS and authorizes deployment.
3. Add verified social URLs and business contact.
4. Review privacy, terms, accessibility, and local legal requirements.
5. Test mobile, desktop, performance, and accessibility.
6. Publish only after owner approval.

GitHub Pages can be evaluated as a low-cost static hosting option; WordPress remains a practical CMS option for a larger article library. No hosting has been provisioned.

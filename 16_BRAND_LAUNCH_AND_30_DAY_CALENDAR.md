## 1. Brand identity system

### Core description
WHAT IF WORLD is a cinematic science and mystery media brand exploring extraordinary possibilities through evidence-led storytelling. Each episode begins with a question, tests the premise against real mechanisms, and separates what is known from what is imagined.

### Short bio (TikTok / Instagram)
**Sains, misteri, dan masa depan dalam cerita sinematik. Jelajahi kemungkinan. Pahami faktanya.**

### Alternative short bio
**Bagaimana jika dunia berubah? Cerita sinematik tentang tubuh, Bumi, teknologi, dan masa depan—berbasis sains.**

### YouTube channel description
WHAT IF WORLD explores the impossible to understand the real.

We create cinematic science, mystery, and future documentaries about the human body, Earth and the cosmos, civilization, and emerging technology. Every story begins with a question and follows the evidence. Facts, extrapolations, and fictional assumptions are clearly distinguished.

Bahasa utama: Indonesia. Selected episodes include English subtitles as we build a global audience.

### Website hero copy
**Explore the Impossible. Understand the Real.**

Extraordinary questions. Evidence-led answers. Cinematic stories about the human body, our planet, civilization, and the future.

Primary CTA: **Watch the latest story**  
Secondary CTA: **Work with our studio**

### Brand voice
- Curious, intelligent, cinematic.
- Confident when evidence is strong; explicit about uncertainty.
- Dramatic without panic or false certainty.
- Clear, accessible Indonesian; avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Never condescending or clickbait that misrepresents the story.

### Visual direction
- Base palette: midnight navy, graphite, cool white.
- Accent: restrained electric cyan or warm amber, not both in every asset.
- Typography: modern sans-serif with strong mobile readability.
- Logo: simple wordmark; avoid generic AI brain/robot symbols.
- Profile image: high-contrast monogram or compact wordmark that remains legible at small size.
- Cover system: one central subject, one short question, strong contrast, consistent typography.
- Do not finalize logo or brand colors until actual design mockups are reviewed.

## 4. Hook testing matrix

For each researched episode, prepare up to three hook treatments:
1. **Visual anomaly:** show an impossible or counterintuitive image first.
2. **Human consequence:** begin with what changes for one person.
3. **Contradiction:** state a surprising but supportable contrast.

Do not test all variables simultaneously. Keep the body of the episode stable when comparing hooks where practical. Compare results against the account's own baseline.

## 5. Community and search loop

After publishing:
- Read genuine questions and confusion points.
- Convert recurring questions into research seeds.
- Pin a question that invites reasoned discussion.
- Reply with evidence, not invented certainty.
- Do not manufacture controversy or bait medical self-experimentation.
- Track which comments reveal search intent or missing explanations.

## 6. Studio service offer architecture

These are proposed service categories, not priced offers or claims of an existing client portfolio.

### Offer A — Research-led Short Video
- Brief and audience objective.
- Source research and claim review.
- Script and storyboard.
- Visual prompt / asset plan.
- Edit and subtitle package.
- Platform metadata and delivery files.

### Offer B — Cinematic Explainer Package
- Deeper research.
- 60–180 second explainer or a set of short episodes.
- Visual system and continuity.
- Voice, sound, and edit direction.
- Rights/provenance documentation.

### Offer C — Documentary Brand Story
- Company/product story development.
- Interview/research structure.
- Documentary script and visual plan.
- Multi-platform cutdown plan.
- QA and delivery.

Before quoting prices, calculate labor, model credits, voice, music, stock, editing, revisions, licensing, taxes, and margin. Never promise a production method or turnaround that has not been tested.

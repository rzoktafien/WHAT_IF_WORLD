## 1. Creative brief and production constraints
- **Core question:** Seberapa parah lapisan ozon bisa rusak jika dunia tidak pernah menyepakati Protokol Montreal?

- **Audience:** Audiens Indonesia, short-form science documentary.
- **Platform/master:** vertical 9:16; 60 seconds target; 1080x1920 recommended editorial master (not a claim about Google Flow output support).
- **Visual language:** realistic cinematic/documentary, scientific visualization with restrained color, naturalistic motion; no disaster spectacle.
- **Emotional curve:** anomaly → understanding → historical choice → alternate-world unease → uncertainty → measured hope.
- **Narrative rule:** 2026 observation, 2009 model scenario, and WMO recovery projection must remain separate data contexts.
- **What this video is not claiming:** no literal hole; no ozone hole over Indonesia; no policy reversal inferred from 2026 seasonal area; no equation of ozone depletion with climate change; no deterministic model forecast.

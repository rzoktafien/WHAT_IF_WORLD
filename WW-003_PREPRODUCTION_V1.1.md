## 1. Creative brief
**Core question:** Bagaimana jika permukaan lahan gambut tampak tenang, tetapi pembakaran membara masih berlangsung di bawahnya?

**Premise:** hypothetical localized subsurface smouldering in peat while the surface appears subdued. This is an educational scenario, not a claim about a current fire or a specific site.

**Audience takeaway:** a calm-looking surface alone does not fully describe subsurface peat conditions; field assessment and appropriate monitoring matter.

**Editorial label:** aktual-terbatas / science-led hypothetical.
**Not claiming:** that all peat fires survive rain, that every hotspot is a peat fire, or that the experiment represents every peatland.

## 4. Scientific model and red-team limits
- Mechanism: smouldering combustion in organic peat, distinct from flaming combustion.
- Spatial scope: localized peat profile; no claim about an entire landscape.
- Time: gradual persistence, not instantaneous underground ignition across a forest.
- Rain nuance: observed response depends on experimental conditions, depth, moisture, fuel structure and suppression; the experiment does not establish a universal rule.
- Visual red lines: no lava/magma, no underground cave, no glowing river, no full-ground fire front, no dramatic eruption, no depiction as verified news footage.
- Cutaway visuals are hypothetical educational illustrations and must be labelled “ILUSTRASI SKENARIO” in the edit where needed.

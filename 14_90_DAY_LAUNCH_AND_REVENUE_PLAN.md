# WHAT IF WORLD — 90-Day Launch & Revenue Plan

**Status:** Approved strategic execution plan  
**Start date:** 2026-11-04  
**Planning horizon:** 90 days  
**Primary market:** Indonesia; global-ready packaging  
**Platforms:** TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, website  
**Business tracks:** media, production studio, IP

## 1. The 90-day objective

Build the minimum viable media operation, publish a meaningful first catalogue, establish a repeatable production system, and test the first realistic revenue paths.

This plan does not promise a specific follower count, viral reach, or income. Those outcomes depend on audience response, platform distribution, execution quality, eligibility, and commercial demand.

### Deliverables by day 90
- Brand identity and editorial system documented.
- Profiles reserved/standardized across TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram.
- Website MVP with About, Watch, Research/Editorial Standards, Contact/Business Enquiries, and privacy/contact disclosures.
- 48 short-form master episodes as a planning target, adapted to TikTok, Shorts, and Reels.
- 4–5 longer YouTube documentaries, subject to research and production capacity.
- One flagship pilot with complete evidence, production, rights, and QA records.
- Basic analytics dashboard and weekly editorial review.
- Studio service portfolio with 3–5 strong sample works or case studies.
- First commercial outreach campaign and a record of responses.
- Monetization eligibility and policy review for each platform.

Targets are operating commitments, not performance guarantees. Reduce volume rather than lower evidence, rights, or production standards.

## 2. Workstream ownership

### AI-led execution
- Research and source verification.
- Topic discovery, scoring, and editorial recommendations.
- Scripts, shot lists, visual prompts, subtitle and caption drafts.
- Continuity and claim-ledger checks.
- Repurposing plans, metadata, QA checklists.
- Performance analysis when real account data is connected or supplied.
- Website copy, service descriptions, pitch materials, and commercial research.

### Human-owner decisions
- Account ownership, credentials, identity verification, payout and tax details.
- Final release approval.
- Paid partnership approval and contract signature.
- Budget approvals and paid-tool subscriptions.
- Legal and rights exceptions.
- Public statements made in the owner's name.

Do not share passwords or one-time codes in chat. Use official account connection flows.

## 3. 90-day phases

### Phase 0 — Foundation (Days 1–7)
**Outputs**
- Confirm official brand name, handles, profile image, banner, bio, and contact identity.
- Create or confirm dedicated brand email and secure account ownership.
- Set up TikTok, YouTube, Instagram professional/creator presence.
- Decide website platform and domain after availability and cost checks.
- Create a master content workspace and naming convention.
- Finalize visual identity and audio direction.
- Review V3.2 rights/provenance and commercial-use gates.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Owner controls all accounts and recovery methods.
- Consistent brand identity across channels.
- No unverified commercial license claims.
- First pilot has a complete research dossier and production plan.

### Phase 1 — Pilot and format validation (Days 8–30)
**Publishing target:** 3–4 short videos per week, across the three lead pillars.

Run a balanced test:
- HUMAN BODY & MIND.
- EARTH & COSMOS.
- CIVILIZATION & FUTURE.

For each topic, test different opening strategies without changing too many variables at once:
- Visual anomaly first.
- Human consequence first.
- Question / contradiction first.

**First pilot:** “Bagaimana Jika Otak Berhenti Mengirim Sinyal Haus?” The research dossier already exists; recheck its evidence and translate it into the first full production package. Keep the distinction between isolated impaired thirst perception and typical adipsic AVP deficiency explicit. Do not present the fictional 24-hour premise as a medically established timeline.

**Acceptance criteria**
- At least 12 published shorts if production and account setup permit.
- All posts have approved captions, disclosure settings where required, and rights records.
- Record baseline metrics; do not declare a winning format from one post.

### Phase 2 — Repeatable production and audience learning (Days 31–60)
**Publishing target:** 4 shorts per week; begin one long-form documentary every two weeks if the first production workflow is stable.

**Actions**
- Double down on the strongest pillar and strongest narrative format, based on actual account metrics.
- Keep a controlled minority of experiments to avoid overfitting to one early result.
- Create a recurring series identity and consistent episode packaging.
- Launch website MVP and a simple email/newsletter capture mechanism only after privacy and consent language is ready.
- Build a concise studio portfolio using owned or properly licensed work.
- Prepare a one-page media kit with actual metrics only; if the channel is too new, label it as a launch portfolio, not a performance media kit.

### Phase 3 — Monetization experiments (Days 61–90)
**Actions**
- Approach a small, relevant set of potential sponsors and production clients with tailored proposals.
- Offer clearly scoped production services: research-led short video, explainer package, documentary-style brand story, or social-video adaptation.
- Test relevant affiliate links only where the product is genuinely useful and disclosures are clear.
- Publish long-form YouTube episodes that can accumulate search and watch time.
- Review platform monetization eligibility inside each official dashboard.
- Evaluate whether the audience is asking for downloadable guides, educational packs, membership, or other paid products. Do not build a product without demand evidence.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A documented list of qualified prospects and outreach outcomes.
- At least one paid pilot proposal ready for negotiation; no income is assumed until a signed deal and payment occur.
- Monetization routes ranked by actual eligibility, audience fit, margin, and execution cost.

## 4. Weekly operating cadence

| Day | Main work |
|---|---|
| Monday | Fresh intelligence, idea scoring, weekly editorial decision |
| Tuesday | Research, claim ledger, script, story structure |
| Wednesday | Visual development, generation tests, voice and sound |
| Thursday | Edit, subtitles, QA, platform adaptation |
| Friday | Publish and community response; prepare next batch |
| Saturday | Long-form / website companion / portfolio work |
| Sunday | Metrics review, lessons, recovery, next-week plan |

This is a planning cadence, not a requirement to publish every day. Batch work where it improves consistency.

## 5. Metrics and decision rules

Record per post:
- Platform, post ID, date/time, pillar, franchise, duration.
- Hook type and opening visual.
- Views / reach / impressions as reported by the platform.
- Average watch time and completion or retention data where available.
- Shares, saves, comments, profile visits, follows.
- Search traffic or search terms where available.
- Production hours and direct cost.
- Rights and AI-disclosure status.

### Weekly analysis
1. Compare each post with the channel's own recent baseline.
2. Identify the strongest and weakest hooks, not only the strongest topics.
3. Inspect retention drop-off points where analytics expose them.
4. Separate distribution failure from creative failure where evidence permits.
5. Record qualitative comment questions as future research leads.
6. Change one or two variables per test cycle.
7. Keep an experiment log; do not treat correlation as proof of causation.

No invented benchmarks or account analytics. Early targets are learning targets, not claims about likely platform performance.

## 6. Revenue architecture

Revenue should be diversified. The order below prioritizes realistic paths that do not require waiting for massive platform reach.

### A. Production studio services — first commercial path to test
Potential offers:
- Research-led short-form video packages.
- Science / technology explainers.
- Documentary-style company or product stories.
- Multi-platform adaptation packages.
- Script, storyboard, visual prompt, and post-production planning.

Requirements:
- Strong sample portfolio.
- Clear deliverables, revisions, schedule, and usage rights.
- Cost model covering labor, AI credits, music, stock, editing, taxes, and contingency.
- Written contract and payment terms.
- No use of third-party assets without appropriate rights.

### B. Sponsorship and brand partnerships
Potential categories must fit the editorial identity: education, science, technology, productivity, books, learning platforms, and relevant consumer products.

Rules:
- Preserve editorial independence.
- Do not make unsupported product claims.
- Clearly disclose commercial content using the platform's required setting and applicable local rules.
- Separate factual editorial conclusions from paid messaging.
- Reject sponsors that undermine trust or require misleading content.

### C. YouTube monetization
As checked 2026-11-04, YouTube's published expanded YPP entry path includes 500 subscribers, 3 valid public uploads in 90 days, and either 3,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or 3 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days for eligible fan-funding / shopping features. Full ad-revenue eligibility is published as 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualified public Shorts views in 90 days. Eligibility and feature availability must be checked in YouTube Studio.

**Important 2027 change:** Google/YouTube announced that from 2027-02-01 new creators applying for ad and Premium revenue sharing will face revised thresholds of 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days. The same announcement describes additional Shorts and brand-deal / Shopping incentives; details may evolve. Recheck the official policy before applying.

Do not build the business around Shorts ad revenue alone. Long-form documentaries create a separate watch-time and catalogue strategy.

### D. TikTok monetization
Creator Rewards has published eligibility conditions including age 18+, a personal account in good standing, at least 10,000 followers, at least 100,000 video views in the previous 30 days, and residence in a supported region. The official announcement does not establish Indonesia as an eligible region. Treat Creator Rewards as **unconfirmed for this account and region** until the official in-app creator tools show availability. Do not forecast this revenue.

Other possible routes include disclosed brand partnerships and platform-supported commerce/affiliate features where the account and region are eligible.

### E. Instagram monetization
Meta announced Instagram Gifts expansion to Indonesia, with published conditions including a professional account, age 18+, compliance with monetization policies, and at least 5,000 followers at the time of that announcement. Product availability and thresholds can change; verify current eligibility in the Professional Dashboard. Brand partnerships may be possible earlier if a relevant audience and portfolio exist.

### F. Owned products and IP
Only after audience demand is visible:
- Downloadable explainers and learning packs.
- Paid research-backed guides.
- Membership / supporter community.
- Licensing of original series concepts and commissioned documentary formats.
- Longer documentary or educational IP adapted for partners.

Do not sell medical, financial, or other high-stakes advice as a by-product of entertainment content.

## 7. Revenue priority by maturity

| Stage | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | Portfolio + studio service validation | Can create revenue before platform thresholds if a client is secured |
| Early audience | Relevant sponsorships and carefully selected affiliate offers | Requires trust and transparent disclosure |
| Growing catalogue | YouTube YPP and platform-native features | Depends on eligibility and qualified views/watch time |
| Returning audience | Digital products / membership | Requires proven audience demand |
| Mature brand | IP licensing, commissioned series, distribution partnerships | Requires distinctive assets, rights clarity, and track record |

## 8. Cost and cash discipline

Before buying tools or ads, maintain a simple monthly ledger:
- Software subscriptions and usage credits.
- Voice, music, stock, and font costs.
- Domain, hosting, email, and storage.
- Contractor / editing costs.
- Paid promotion.
- Taxes, transaction fees, and payment processing.
- Revenue by source and payment status.

Calculate contribution margin per project:
**Revenue received − direct production cost − platform/payment fees − attributable contractor cost.**

Do not count promised, pending, or unpaid deals as realized revenue. Do not use paid views as evidence of organic audience demand.

## 9. Rights, originality, and platform integrity

- Keep prompt, source, model/version, asset provenance, music, voice, and license records.
- Do not reuse copyrighted film/TV footage or music without a valid basis.
- Do not clone voices or likenesses without explicit rights and permission.
- Label realistic AI-generated or significantly AI-altered media where required.
- Avoid mass-produced, minimally transformed, repetitive content. Each episode must have substantive original research, narrative, explanation, editing, and editorial value.
- Commercial direction recorded in the repository is not a license grant. The rights and licensing gates in V3.2 remain mandatory.
